Community Guidelines

CINEMIX aims to provide a respectful and enjoyable environment for everyone. We expect all users to follow these guidelines when using interactive features of our platform.

1. Be Respectful

Treat other users with respect. Harassment, threats and abusive behaviour are not acceptable.

2. No Hate or Discrimination

Do not post content that promotes hatred, discrimination or violence against individuals or groups.

3. No Spam

Avoid repetitive, misleading or promotional messages.

4. Respect Copyright

Do not upload, submit or share copyrighted materials without appropriate authorization.

5. No Harmful Activities

Do not attempt to exploit, damage or disrupt CINEMIX or its users.

6. Report Violations

If you encounter content or behaviour that violates these guidelines, report it to our team.

7. Enforcement

We may review reported activity and take appropriate action, including removing content or restricting access to features, where permitted by applicable law.
