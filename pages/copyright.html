Copyright Policy

CINEMIX respects intellectual property rights and expects users and content contributors to do the same. We recognize the importance of protecting the rights of creators, studios, publishers and copyright owners.

1. Ownership of Content

All third-party videos, images, trademarks, characters and other copyrighted materials belong to their respective owners. CINEMIX does not claim ownership of such materials unless explicitly stated.

2. DMCA & Copyright Complaints

If you believe that material available through CINEMIX infringes your copyright, contact us with:

  • Your full legal name and contact email address.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work.
  • The exact URL of the allegedly infringing material.
  • An explanation of your copyright ownership or authorization.
  • A good-faith statement that the information provided is accurate.
  • A statement confirming you are authorized to act on behalf of the rights owner, where applicable.

Although the DMCA is a United States law, we provide this process to explain how to raise a copyright concern. Applicable copyright laws in other jurisdictions, including India, may also apply.

3. Review Process

We will review properly submitted complaints and may take appropriate action, including removing or disabling access to disputed material where necessary.

4. Third-Party Embedded Content

If a video is hosted on YouTube or another third-party platform, you may also contact that platform through its official copyright reporting process.

5. Counter-Notification

Where applicable, affected users or content providers may submit a response explaining why the material should remain available.
