Accessibility Statement

At CINEMIX, we believe that everyone should have the opportunity to explore and enjoy entertainment comfortably. We aim to make our website accessible and easy to navigate for as many users as possible.

Our Accessibility Goals

We aim to improve:

  • Website navigation.
  • Text readability and colour contrast.
  • Keyboard accessibility.
  • Clear page structure.
  • Compatibility with commonly used browsers and assistive technologies.

Continuous Improvements

We understand that accessibility is an ongoing process. We will continue reviewing and improving the website experience.
